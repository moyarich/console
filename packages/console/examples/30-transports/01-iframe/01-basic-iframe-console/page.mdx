## Receiving options and cleanup

`listenForConsolePostMessages` requires `events`. Its optional `channel` defaults to `"default"`; `origin` accepts a string, RegExp, or predicate; `source` matches a specific message source; and `targetWindow` defaults to the current window. The returned function removes the listener. Call it when the receiver is torn down.

The listener validates source, origin, envelope, and channel before deserializing and delivering the event. Transport constants are `CONSOLE_TRANSPORT_TYPE` (`"CONSOLE_PANEL"`), `CONSOLE_TRANSPORT_VERSION` (`1`), and `DEFAULT_CONSOLE_CHANNEL` (`"default"`). `isConsoleEnvelope` validates shape without deserializing.
